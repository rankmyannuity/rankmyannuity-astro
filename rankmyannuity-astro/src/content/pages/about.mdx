## What this site is

RankMyAnnuity.pro publishes independent reviews and rankings of fixed and fixed
indexed annuities. Every ranking is tied to a documented methodology, and every
carrier and product review carries a date, a reviewer, and source links.

## Who runs it

The site is operated by a single editorial principal with a Series 65 registration
and a long background in financial services. Research is the product; there is
no lead-routing business and no CRM pipeline behind the scenes.

## How it's funded

During Phase 2 of the migration the site carries no ads, no affiliate links, and
no paid placements. When advertising is introduced, it will be disclosed on a
dedicated disclosure page and will never determine editorial ordering of rankings
or the grades in reviews.

## What this site will not do

- Will not collect user account data or build user accounts.
- Will not route leads to insurance agents.
- Will not rank products in exchange for compensation.

## Corrections

If you spot an error, or a rating, cap, or fee that has changed since our last
