## About this carrier

New York Life Insurance and Annuity Corporation (NYLIAC) is a
U.S. individual-annuity issuer domiciled in Delaware. In LIMRA's
Fourth Quarter 2025 Top 20 Annuity Sales Rankings, New York Life
ranked #2 after Athene, with $30.61 billion in reported total
individual-annuity sales for the period. [1]

## Rate observations

As of 2026-04-22 observation, New York Life's Secure Term MVA II
5-year rate at the $100,000+ premium band was 4.60% per the
carrier's published rate sheet, effective 2026-04-27. [2] On the
same sheet, as of 2026-04-22 observation, New York Life's Secure
Term Choice II 5-year rate at the $100,000+ premium band was
4.50%, effective 2026-04-27. [3] The carrier publishes a
future-dated sheet — the sheet carries an effective date five
days after the 2026-04-22 observation window — which is a fact
about the sheet's publication cadence rather than a prediction
about the rates it lists.

Both rates sit on a premium band that the carrier caps at
$1,499,999; NYL discloses that premium amounts of $1.5 million
or more are handled outside the published band and require
NYLIAC approval. [2]

## Corpus comparison

Of the three 5-year MYGA rates captured in the Phase 6 corpus,
we observed Pacific Life's Pacific Harbor published at the
highest rate — 5.15% at the $200,000+ premium band, effective
2026-04-16 — as of 2026-04-22 observation. [4] New York Life's
two 5-year variants (4.60% MVA II and 4.50% Choice II, both at
the $100,000+ premium band) sit below the Pacific Harbor row on
the corpus's ranked-by-published-rate axis; the two carriers'
premium bands differ, so the comparison is a published-rate
comparison at each carrier's respective disclosed band rather
than a like-for-like premium-band comparison.

## Product scope

This carrier review covers two New York Life Secure Term
variants — MVA II and Choice II — both 5-year MYGAs with a
market-value adjustment allowed per the product frontmatter,
and both captured on the same carrier rate sheet as of
2026-04-22 observation. The linked Secure Term MYGA product
review treats the MVA II and Choice II rate rows as the
variants of that product family and carries its own
independently numbered rate citations against the same rate
sheet. Additional New York Life products are out of scope for
wave-1 and are not represented in this review.
